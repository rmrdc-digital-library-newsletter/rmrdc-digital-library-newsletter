Institutional concept

Research-to-Industry Intelligence Platform

A proposed RMRDC information service that strengthens the connection between research outputs, raw-material intelligence, industrial needs and investment decisions.

What the platform is intended to do

The platform moves beyond a conventional digital repository. It is designed to organise RMRDC research and technology information around the questions investors and industrial organisations ask when assessing opportunities.

How it should be developed

The first stage is requirements discovery. The Library and Investment Department collaborate to identify actual investor information needs, decision factors and information gaps. These validated requirements then guide the information architecture, matching criteria and future software development.

Core intelligence areas

  • Technology readiness and technical evidence
  • Production economics and scale-up requirements
  • Raw-material availability and supply information
  • Market, demand and import-substitution potential
  • Intellectual property and licensing information
  • Researcher, technology-owner and engagement pathways
