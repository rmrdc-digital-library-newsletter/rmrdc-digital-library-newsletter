# RMRDC v3.4.7 Free Demo EOI Update

- The two investor demo opportunities marked `data-demo-free="true"` remain accessible to free users.
- Free demo users can open **Express Interest** without subscribing.
- The EOI form has a clear **Send Expression of Interest to RMRDC** button.
- Submission writes a structured `investor_requests` record including technology title, investor details, interest type, investment range, preferred next step, timeline, and message.
- The request status is `received` for RMRDC Investment/Admin review.
- Run `sql/SUPABASE_RTI_V3_4_7_DEMO_EOI.sql` in Supabase to allow authenticated investors to insert their own requests under RLS.
- Existing subscriber gating for all non-demo technologies remains unchanged.
